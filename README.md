# AI Pop Video

AI Pop Video is a web-based photo-to-video generator for short dance clips and visual effects. This repository provides a product overview and practical notes for using the website.

## Website and tools

- **Homepage:** [AI Pop Video](https://aipopvideo.com/) — explore photo-to-video templates and available effects.
- **Featured template:** [Bulin 47 AI Video Generator](https://aipopvideo.com/bulin-47-ai-video-generator) — create a freestyle-inspired video from one photo.

## Getting started

1. Visit the homepage and choose a template that fits your idea.
2. Read the template's photo requirements and current credit cost.
3. Upload a clear photo that you have permission to use.
4. Generate the video, review the result, and download it.

Available templates include dance, pet, baby, and cartoon effects. Requirements, clip duration, and credit cost depend on the selected template; check its page before generating.

## Bulin 47 freestyle template

The Bulin 47 template is inspired by the street freestyle scene associated with the "Dame Hombro" shoulder move. It turns a single photo into a vertical video with a red-lit doorway, a surrounding crowd, and a freestyle performance setting.

## Photo tips

Choose a well-lit image with a clearly visible subject facing the camera. Avoid heavy filters, sunglasses, and crowded group photos. Follow the selected template's guidance when preparing your image.
